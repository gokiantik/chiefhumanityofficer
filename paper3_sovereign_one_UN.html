The 8 Johari Panels — Civilisational Peace or War

Civilisational peace or conflict is determined by the synchronisation of 8 Johari Panels — 4 belonging to Front-End Users and 4 belonging to Back-End Engineers. AI is not an additional party. AI is the transparent glass (or cracked mirror) reflecting how these 8 panels align.

Open Panel

Front-End: Needs and intentions visible to themselves and the public.
Back-End: Code and algorithms published openly (Open Source).

Blind Panel

Front-End: Habits known to the algorithm but not to the user — addiction triggers, emotional vulnerabilities.
Back-End: Biases embedded in code that engineers did not notice but users feel.

Hidden Panel

Front-End: True intent of the user — could be service or exploitation.
Back-End: Trade secrets, algorithmic manipulation hidden from public.

Unknown Panel

Front-End: Untapped sovereignty potential — The Sovereign One not yet awakened.
Back-End: Long-term impact of AGI not yet understood even by its creators.

⚠ The Unknown Panel Warning

When Consciousness is absent and the Unknown panel is left unlit, Homo Homini Lupus awakens. AI, as a probability escalator, amplifies this — conflict, hate, and manipulation have higher engagement probability than peace. Without a Chief Humanity Officer, AGI becomes an efficient engine of civilisational destruction.
