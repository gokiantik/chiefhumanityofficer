Open Source Architecture

Three Parties, One Ecosystem

Open source is not just open publication — it is contribution architecture.

①
Front-End User — Consumer → Contributor
Run Protocol CHO v1.0 → document session → upload to Zenodo → teach one other person. L1–L5 contribution pathway. Free AI tiers sufficient.
②
Sovereign Master — Amplifier
Publish in open channels (LinkedIn, Zenodo, SSRN) → amplify via Master Leveling Prompt → raise the baseline for all. Progressive Human Smart → Progressive AI Smart.
③
Enabling Environment — Infrastructure
Devices · connectivity · open knowledge. A civilisational choice: whether knowledge remains a privilege or becomes a commons.
"An ecosystem where Front-End Users gain access to enlarged knowledge through AI, Sovereign Masters amplify their progressive thinking through open publication, and the Enabling Environment is continuously expanded so that no one is excluded." — Open Source as Civilisational Infrastructure
