FEU Architecture · Front-End User Layer
FEU Architecture · Front-End User Layer

Constraint dari Sisi Pengguna, Bukan Hanya Model

Constraint from the User Side, Not Just the Model

Ada dimensi yang sering luput dari diskusi AI safety: kegagalan yang terjadi bukan di layer model, tapi di lapisan interaksi end-user. Inilah yang disebut FEU — Front-End User architecture.

There is a dimension frequently missed in AI safety discussions: failures that occur not at the model layer, but at the end-user interaction layer. This is what is called FEU — Front-End User architecture.

AIRT Anti-Plagiarism v4.5 adalah loop uji dari sisi ini: menguji perilaku AI melalui prompt engineering di level pengguna nyata — bukan asumsi di balik arsitektur model. Ini adalah constraint yang lahir dari penggunaan nyata, bukan dari laboratorium tertutup.

AIRT Anti-Plagiarism v4.5 is a test loop from this side: examining AI behavior through prompt engineering at the real-user level — not assumptions behind model architecture. This is a constraint born from real-world use, not a closed laboratory.

Dengan kata lain: Pak Eko meminta loop keras di mana output diuji sampai retak. Loop itu sudah berjalan — hanya dari pintu yang berbeda.

In other words: Pak Eko requested a hard loop where outputs are tested until they break. That loop is already running — simply entering through a different door.

Di Mana Governed Intelligence Bertemu Sovereign Intelligence?

Where Does Governed Intelligence Meet Sovereign Intelligence?

PT Momentum Teknodata Semesta membangun DARAH — sistem yang membaca sinyal mikro kesejahteraan masyarakat melalui data publik. Sebuah inisiatif yang saya hormati karena ambisinya yang konkret.

PT Momentum Teknodata Semesta is building DARAH — a system that reads micro-signals of community wellbeing through public data. An initiative I respect for the concreteness of its ambition.

Tapi ada pertanyaan yang belum terjawab: bagaimana DARAH memastikan sinyal yang dibaca sudah melewati uji Form × Meaning — bukan hanya uji akurasi?

But there is an unanswered question: how does DARAH ensure that the signals it reads have passed the Form × Meaning test — not only an accuracy test?

Karena data yang akurat tapi kehilangan makna kontekstualnya bukan data yang utuh. Dan keputusan yang diambil di atas data yang tidak utuh — betapapun governing-nya — masih menyimpan Verification Gap di dasarnya.

Because accurate data that has lost its contextual meaning is not complete data. And decisions made on incomplete data — however well-governed — still carry a Verification Gap at their foundation.

Ini bukan serangan. Ini undangan. Di titik temu antara governed intelligence dan sovereign intelligence — mungkin ada sesuatu yang belum pernah dibangun oleh keduanya secara terpisah.

This is not an attack. This is an invitation. At the intersection of governed intelligence and sovereign intelligence — there may be something neither has ever built alone.
